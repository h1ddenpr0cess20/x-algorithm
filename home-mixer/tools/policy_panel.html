Feed Policy Console

home-mixer · FeedPolicyScorer + FeedQualityFilter

Network

Applied first, by where the post came from. Unknown means in-network hydration never landed — those posts are dropped by the filter before they reach here.

Category

Multiplied together, so a post tagged both sports and fandom takes both cuts. Hard news stacks on top of everything. Art, music, nature and history are one boost in four parts. Religion, philosophy and platitude posting are one cut in three parts. Within either group, an overlap takes only the strongest single weight. Text matching is conservative; history stays off hard news.

Prediction

How much of the model's predicted-engagement spread survives into the order. The top of the batch keeps its score and everything below is drawn toward it, so the weights decide more and the tap prediction less. Order within the model's own ranking is untouched.

Affiliation

Author-level cuts for X / xAI / Tesla / SpaceX affiliates, current Trump-administration accounts, and foreign-government accounts. Reposts inherit the source classification. When rules overlap, only the strongest single cut applies. State-controlled media is outside the government roster.

Overexposure

An author whose share of Elon mentions in this batch exceeds the threshold gets cut. Needs at least 4 posts from that author in the batch before it measures anything.

Hard filters

Removal, not ranking. These run in FeedQualityFilter before any score is computed.
